When studies are open.

Studies.

First come, first served. A study closes when it reaches its number of participants.

How you get credit.

Four steps, and your study answers never carry your name.

  1. Join the pool once per term.The sign-up form asks for your name, FSU ID and the participating courses you are taking, then gives you a Pool ID such as 27JAN10_130042. Save it.
  2. Pick a study and open its link.Read the consent page. You can stop at any point and still get credit for the time you spent.
  3. Use the credit link when you finish or stop.It opens a separate short form for your Pool ID and the course you want the credit in. Your study answers are never stored with your name.
  4. Your instructor sees only your total.The pool administrator sends each instructor students' credit totals after each window, never which studies you took.

One course per study

If you are in two participating courses, credit from a study counts toward only one of them. Do not take the same study twice.

Prefer not to take part?

You can earn the same credit through an alternative assignment set by your instructor. Check your syllabus; it is due by the end of Window 2.

About the research pool.

Please read this before taking part. Each study also has its own consent page explaining what you will be asked to do.

Taking part is voluntary

You do not have to take part in any study. If you prefer not to, you can earn the same credit through an alternative assignment set by your instructor. Choosing not to take part will not affect your grade or your relationship with your instructor or the department.

Who can take part

Students 18 or older enrolled in a participating Management course. Students under 18 earn credit through the alternative assignment.

What the forms collect

Joining asks for your name, FSU ID and participating courses and gives you a Pool ID, a code based on when you joined. Start study checks your Pool ID, then opens the study. The credit form records your Pool ID, the study and your course, and gives you a completion ID as a receipt. No form records your IP address or location.

Who sees it

Each study receives only your Pool ID, never your name, and stores it with your answers. Only the pool coordinator and administrator can match a Pool ID to a name, and they never share that list, including with researchers. Your instructor receives only your name, FSU ID and total credit, not which studies you took.

How long it is kept

The list linking Pool IDs to names and all credit records are deleted after final grades are posted and the grade appeal period ends, no later than the end of the following semester. After that, a Pool ID in a study's data can no longer be linked to anyone. You may ask the coordinator to remove you from the pool at any time.

Risks

Minimal. The main risk would be someone using the pool's list to connect your Pool ID to your study answers. The list is kept only by the pool coordinator and administrator in restricted FSU systems and is deleted each term.

Questions about your rights as a research participant can go to the FSU Office for Human Subjects Protection. Joining the pool means you have read this information and agree to have your credit recorded as described.

Questions or missing credit.

Participation is voluntary. Students must be 18 or older to take part. Faculty do not recruit their own current students in person for their own studies. Department of Management, Florida State University.
