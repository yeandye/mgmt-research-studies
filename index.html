Research Studies, Spring 2027

Take part in faculty and doctoral research for course credit. Every study is reviewed by FSU's Institutional Review Board, runs online, and takes about 30 minutes.

Each 30 minutes earns 0.5% Your course offers up to 1% One 30-minute session per window

When studies are open

Studies

Studies are first come, first served. A study closes when it reaches its number of participants.

How you get credit

  1. Join the pool once per term. The sign-up form asks for your name, FSU ID and the participating courses you are taking, then gives you a Pool ID such as 27JAN10_130042. Save it.
  2. Pick a study and open its link. Read the consent page. You can stop at any point and still get credit for the time you spent.
  3. Use the credit link when you finish or stop. It opens a separate short form for your Pool ID and the course you want the credit in. Your study answers are never stored with your name.
  4. Your instructor sees only your total. The pool administrator sends each instructor students' credit totals after each window, never which studies you took.

One course per study

If you are in two participating courses, credit from a study counts toward only one of them. Do not take the same study twice.

Prefer not to take part?

You can earn the same credit through an alternative assignment set by your instructor. Check your syllabus; it is due by the end of Window 2.

Questions or missing credit

Participation is voluntary. Students under 18 cannot take part unless a study is approved for minors. Faculty do not recruit their own current students in person for their own studies.
